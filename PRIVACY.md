# Privacy policy

Firaw — Transcrição OBS processes recordings, audio, recognized text and visual
speaker hints locally on the user's computer. The application does not upload
recordings, transcripts or participant information to Firawynix.

On first use the installer can download public runtime components and speech
models from their official publishers. Those HTTP requests expose the usual
network metadata to the respective download providers. The generated logs and
transcripts remain under the user's control and are not part of this repository.

Questions can be sent to `no-reply@firawynix.com.br`.
